# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in unset.sh's GitHub repositories and community discussions should be able to contribute
without harassment. These expectations apply to maintainers as well as other participants.

## How we work together

- Treat people respectfully, regardless of their background, identity, experience or ability.
- Discuss the work and explain disagreements with evidence. Accept constructive feedback and respect boundaries.
- Keep conversations relevant and make room for questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy. Do not share someone else's personal information or private correspondence without permission.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, impersonation, doxxing and repeated unwanted contact
are not acceptable. Spam and deliberately disruptive participation are also unwelcome.

## Report a concern

Use GitHub's **Report content** option where available, or **Block or report → Report abuse** on an account's profile.
[GitHub's reporting guide](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
explains the available routes. GitHub Support receives abuse reports; an option to report directly to repository
administrators appears only where that repository has enabled it. Do not post sensitive incident details publicly.

For a software vulnerability, use the separate
[security reporting route](https://github.com/unset-sh/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

Maintainers may ask participants to change their behaviour, hide or remove disruptive content, lock a conversation,
or restrict participation. Actions should reflect the conduct and its impact; serious or repeated abuse can lead
to removal from the project's spaces. GitHub separately enforces its platform rules through its reporting process.
