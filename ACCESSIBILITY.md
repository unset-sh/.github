# Accessibility

This is the default accessibility information for unset.sh repositories. Project-specific documentation should
describe tested environments, known barriers and available alternatives for that project.

## Priorities

We aim to make interfaces and documentation usable with a keyboard and assistive technology, with clear text,
meaningful labels, readable contrast and layouts that remain usable when zoomed. Accessibility needs belong in the
design and review of a change, alongside its functional behaviour.

This page is not a conformance assessment or a list of supported browser and assistive-technology combinations.
The absence of a recorded issue does not establish that a page or feature is accessible.

## Report a barrier

Use the affected repository's **Issues → New issue → Accessibility barrier** form when available, or follow its
own reporting instructions. If ownership is unclear, use the
[organisation issue chooser](https://github.com/unset-sh/.github/issues/new/choose).

Describe the task you were trying to complete, the page or component, what prevented you from completing it, and
what you expected. Browser, operating system, assistive technology and zoom settings can help when relevant, but
you do not need to disclose a disability or medical information. Screenshots or recordings are optional; remove
personal data before sharing them.

Maintainers should record confirmed barriers and any available workaround in the affected project's issues or
documentation, and check the reported task when reviewing a fix. For general help, see
[Support](https://github.com/unset-sh/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md).
